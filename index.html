🕷️ Spider-Man & Miles Morales: Flight Combat Adventure

Game Guide & Instructions

📖 The Story

You are two superheroes fighting together in the sky!

🔴 COOLMAN = Spider-Man
  • Superhero with red and blue suit
  • Flies really fast using nanotech thrusters (rocket boosters!)
  • Has a glowing blue shield that protects him
  • Shoots web projectiles to fight bad guys
  • Brave and strong! 💪
🟣 FIREMAN = Miles Morales
  • Another superhero with a purple suit
  • Spider-Man's best friend and partner
  • Shoots fire and electricity at enemies ⚡🔥
  • Fights by your side automatically
  • Helps you defeat all the bad guys!

🎯 What Are You Doing Here?

You're in a sky battle with a set ending!

It's NOT endless - there's a specific goal you're trying to reach:

Enemies attack you in waves across 10 levels, and you need to:

  1. SURVIVE - Don't let enemies hurt you too much
  2. FIGHT BACK - Shoot and destroy all the enemies
  3. COLLECT ENERGY - Pick up blue crystals for power
  4. GET STRONGER - Level up by getting enough points
  5. REACH LEVEL 10 - The final level where you meet the ultimate villain
  6. DEFEAT VENOM - Fight the big boss at Level 10
  7. WIN THE GAME - Beat Venom and the game is over! 🎉

⚙️ How Does It All Work?

The Nanotech System 🤖

Both Spider-Man and Miles have nanotech armor - special high-tech suits that:

  • Let them fly in the sky using thrusters
  • Create projectiles (web shots and electricity bolts) to attack
  • Generate a protective shield around Spider-Man
  • Leave glowing particle effects as they move

The 10 Levels 📈

The game gets harder and harder:

Level Difficulty What Happens
1-3 Easy 😊 Few enemies, lots of time
4-6 Medium 😐 More enemies, faster attacks
7-9 Hard 😰 Many enemies, rapid fire
10 BOSS! 😱 VENOM appears!

Each level needs more points to complete. As you level up:

  • ⚡ Enemies shoot faster
  • 🔴 More obstacles appear
  • 💥 Everything moves quicker
  • 🎯 You need better strategy!

👹 Who Is VENOM?

VENOM is the final boss - the big bad guy you fight at Level 10!

What he does:

  • Stays in the center of the screen
  • Shoots projectiles at you constantly
  • Gets stronger as his health goes down
  • Has 3 different attack phases

How to defeat him:

  • Shoot him with SPACEBAR repeatedly
  • Miles helps you by shooting electricity and fire
  • Use your shield to block his attacks
  • Keep moving so you don't get hit
  • Don't give up! Keep shooting until he's defeated!

When you beat Venom, you see: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ VICTORY!

🔧 The Mechanics - How It All Works

Your Shield 🛡️

  • Protects you from damage
  • Regenerates slowly when you're not being attacked
  • Breaks after taking too much damage
  • When it breaks, you lose a LIFE
  • You start with 3 lives

Three Types of Attacks 💥

1. Spider-Man's Web Shots 🕸️

  • Press SPACEBAR
  • White/yellow projectiles
  • Fast and reliable

2. Miles' Fire Attacks 🔥

  • Miles shoots automatically (25% of the time)
  • Orange/red fireballs
  • Damages enemies

3. Miles' Electricity Attacks ⚡

  • Miles shoots electricity (75% of the time)
  • Bright cyan bolts with electric effects
  • Looks super cool!

Blue Energy Crystals 🟦

  • Float around the screen
  • Collect them by flying over them
  • Give you POINTS!
  • Help you level up faster

Red Enemy Obstacles 🔴

  • Shoot at you constantly
  • You must destroy them
  • They explode when defeated
  • Give you points too!
